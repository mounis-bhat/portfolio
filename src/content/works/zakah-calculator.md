## Overview

A small, focused web app for calculating Zakah — the annual charitable obligation in Islam — across the usual categories of wealth. Built with SvelteKit and deployed as a static site, with a clean layout that works well on a phone.

## Notes

Built with SvelteKit (static adapter), TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. The goal was a frictionless single-purpose tool: enter your holdings, get the amount due, no accounts or tracking.
